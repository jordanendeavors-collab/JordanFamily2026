📦 Shoebox shopping list

Pick an age group (2–4, 5–9, or 10–14) and boy or girl. Use a standard shoebox or a plastic tub.
  • One "wow" item (soccer ball with pump, doll, stuffed animal)
  • School supplies: pencils, sharpener, crayons, notebook
  • Hygiene: toothbrush, bar soap, washcloth, comb
  • Small toys: yo-yo, jump rope, cars, harmonica
  • Clothing: socks, t-shirt, hat
  • A note and photo from your son
Not allowed: candy, liquids, anything war-related, used items. Double-check the current rules on the OCC site before packing.
